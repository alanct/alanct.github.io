About me

I am a Professor of Marketing at the Chinese University of Hong Kong. I teach Marketing Research, Quantitative Marketing and Internet Marketing at both undergraduate and postgraduate levels. My current research interests are data analytics, complexity systems in marketing, and Chinese wisdom and marketing.
